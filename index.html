Zone guide

The ranges below are planning rules of thumb, and they include each machine's clearance. The tool's layouts use the clearances stored with each item in your database. Treadmills default to the ASTM F2115 minimum of 0.5 m (about 1.6 ft) at each side and 2 m (about 6.5 ft) behind. Keep main aisles at least 3 ft wide (36 in, the accessible-route minimum), and use 4–5 ft in commercial gyms. Your own standards and local codes come first.
